## General Questions

Q: What is Crypto AML Bot?

A: Crypto AML Bot is an advanced automated Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and compliance tool for cryptocurrency. It performs real-time risk screening of addresses, transactions, and wallets using leading AML intelligence providers.

Q: Which blockchains does it support?

A: The bot supports multiple blockchains, including:Bitcoin (BTC)
Ethereum (ETH) & EVM-compatible chains
Solana
Tron (TRX)
Binance Smart Chain (BSC)
Polygon, Avalanche, and more.

Q: Is this bot intended for individuals or businesses?

A: It is designed primarily for crypto businesses, exchanges, payment processors, DeFi projects, and compliance teams. However, advanced individual users can also utilize it.Features & Technology

Q: Which AML providers does it integrate with?

A: The bot integrates with top-tier providers:Chainalysis
Scorechain
TRM Labs
Crystal Blockchain
Own internal risk engine (works even without external APIs)

Q: How accurate is the risk scoring?

A: The system combines multiple data sources and assigns a 0–100 risk score with detailed reasoning, including sanctions lists, PEPs, fund flow analysis, and wallet behavior patterns.

Q: Does it support real-time transaction monitoring?

A: Yes. You can monitor specific wallets or smart contracts in real-time and receive instant Telegram or webhook alerts when risky activity is detected.

Q: Can this bot be used for mixing services or privacy coins?

A: The bot can analyze such transactions and will usually assign a high-risk score to them.

Q: How fast are the checks?

A: Most address checks are completed in 1.5 – 4 seconds, depending on the number of enabled data sources.

Q: Do you provide updates and support?

A: Yes. The repository is actively maintained, and new features + provider integrations are added regularly.

Q: Can I run the bot 24/7?

A: Absolutely. It is designed for continuous operation.
